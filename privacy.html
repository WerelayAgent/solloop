Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026What SolLoop collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Wallet address when you connect a wallet, to show your positions and to sign you in for social features.
  • Social activity you create: follows, comments, likes and profile details.
  • Uploaded images such as token images.
  • Technical data such as device type and approximate location from your IP address, for security and analytics.

What we do not collect

We never see or store your private keys or recovery phrase. We do not require your name, email address or identity documents to use the interface.

Public blockchain data

Transactions on the blockchain are public and permanent. SolLoop cannot delete or change them.

How we use data

  • To run the Platform, show token and portfolio data, and enable social features.
  • To detect abuse, fraud and security incidents.
  • To measure and improve the product.

Sharing

We share data with service providers that host the Platform, provide node access and run analytics, and when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

Service providers we use

  • Sentry receives error and performance reports from the website so we can fix bugs. Reports carry no IP address, cookies or user details, and wallet addresses are shortened before a report leaves your browser.
  • Solana Web3 (WalletConnect) connects your wallet when you choose a wallet through it. It sees your wallet address and the connection, not your keys.
  • X: when you apply as a Looper, we look up the public X profile of the handle you enter (followers, account age, bio) to check it. This is read-only. We never post, follow or send messages for you.
  • Anthropic runs the assistant in the live chat. What you write there is sent to Anthropic to produce an answer.
  • Telegram carries our internal alerts about the Platform. When you open a support ticket or ask to talk to a person in the live chat, a short notice that can include the start of your message goes to the support team's Telegram chat.
  • Vercel hosts the website and runs its page analytics and speed measurements (pages visited, device type, approximate location). Secret parts of a link, such as a support ticket's key, are removed before a report is sent.
  • Cloudflare runs our domain's DNS, and requests to our API pass through it. It sees your IP address and the request, to protect the Platform against attacks and abuse.
  • DigitalOcean runs our API servers, database and backups. The data listed above is stored there.
  • Support: support tickets and chat messages are stored on our servers so the team can answer them.

Retention and your choices

We keep social data while your profile exists and delete it on request where we can. Contact admin@solloop.fun to ask about your data. Blockchain records cannot be removed.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new effective date.
